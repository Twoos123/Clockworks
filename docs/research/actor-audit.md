# Actor and weapon audit

Generated by `tools/audit/play-audit.ts` (Playwright, SwiftShader) on cached level 370 in play mode. Each monster is spawned
beside a knight that cannot fall, watched for 8 seconds, struck hard once (it should flinch) and then killed. Each weapon is
equipped and fired through its basic chain (a gun until it reloads) and its charge attack against an inert jelly cube.

| | Before | After |

| Monsters with no problems | 40 of 723 | 552 of 723 |
| Weapons with no problems | 0 of 381 | 370 of 381 |

Fixed (clean-room, from the configs):

- Bullets and bombs drew nothing: their model is the actor's `sprite.model`. Their scripted parts now run (a bomb's drop sound and fuse ring).
- Attacks used only their first fire action: bursts (autogun), volleys (sixshot charge) and thrust-plus-shot (tortofist) now fire every action at its delay. Empty fire actions (a lichen's merge) strike nothing.
- `AnimationConfig$Sequential` clips did not load (every gun charge release, tortofist fire, auto-shell cool-down).
- Attacks with `blended: false` (gun charged shots) play on the whole body; the weapon model plays its own `ks_attack`, `ks_charge`, `ks_charge_attack` and `ks_default` animations; attached models keep their own clocks and automatic animations.
- Detonations run their other actions (lichen colony spawns, heals, markers) and harmless ones deal no damage.
- Monsters flinch and die with their model's `reacting` and `dying` animations (held for the config's `deathTime`); `AttackConfig$Chain` leads into the next attack; quad and speed-matched movement animations; a monster stuck on a ledge paths around it.
- Knight: `death.dat` fall, the shield-hit pose is no longer cut off by the shield pose, `hurt_hard` stagger on major-react hits.
- Lichens fuse (see below); monsters' unnamed signal handlers no longer answer the triggers' "activate"; removing an actor without its shutdown (a fusing lichen) still stops its sensors and timers.

What remains (mostly data the install lacks, or closed-code behaviour):

- Animations and models missing from the install: lizardman and dust bunny animations, the Ironscale Hunter and Beast Master models, razor drone jitter.
- Parts and markers (boss bullets, Vanaduke debris and flame markers, swarm parts) have no idle animation by design and are not standalone monsters; 19 configs are props or orbitals, not monsters.
- Agent-class bullets (diskgun and mixer cores spawning orbital bullets) are not simulated: the 11 weapons that fire them do nothing.
  *Since fixed:* disks, mixer cores and their satellites fly (src/game/orbitals.ts; see
  combat-mechanics.md, "Disk guns and mixers"); `tools/audit/weapon-fire.ts` checks every
  weapon fires. The Pulsar's "no sound" on its charge is the install's data: its charged
  shot's muzzle flare names no sound (other pulsars use `fx_muzzleflare_sound.dat`).
- About 40 monsters do not flinch at a big hit (bosses and armoured monsters whose react threshold the hit did not reach after defence).
